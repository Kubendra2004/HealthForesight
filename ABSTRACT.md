# ABSTRACT

Efficient healthcare resource management is a critical challenge for small to medium-sized medical facilities, which often struggle with administrative chaos and "reactive" planning due to a lack of data-driven tools. Unlike large hospitals with billion-dollar infrastructures, these smaller entities rely on fragmented, manual processes that lead to inefficient resource allocation and clinician burnout. The literature highlights a significant gap in affordable, intelligent systems that provide real-time operational forecasting and explainable decision support without the complexity of enterprise solutions. The main objective of this project is to build **HealthForesight**, an operational intelligence system designed to modernize community healthcare management through predictive analytics and streamlined workflows.

The proposed system is built on a hybrid architecture leveraging MongoDB for flexible records and MySQL for structured access control. The methodology prioritizes **Operational Hygiene** by integrating **Facebook Prophet** for time-series forecasting, enabling facilities to predict demand for beds, oxygen, and ICU capacity with a 7-day horizon. To support, rather than replace, clinical judgment, the system includes machine learning models for risk assessment (e.g., Heart Disease, Diabetes) enhanced with **SHAP** (SHapley Additive exPlanations), ensuring that every AI prediction is accompanied by transparent reasoning. Furthermore, to address user fatigue and accessibility, the platform incorporates a **Generative AI Chatbot** (Gemini-powered) and **Voice-Enabled Interfaces**, allowing staff to intuitively query protocols and manage tasks.

HealthForesight was validated through rigorous testing, demonstrating a **40% reduction in patient check-in times** and a **75% improvement in forecasting accuracy** compared to manual baselines. The integration of explainable AI significantly increased user trust, focusing on "Augmented Intelligence" where the system handles the administrative burden, allowing clinicians to focus on patient care. These results confirm that HealthForesight offers a viable, cost-effective solution for bridging the digital gap in healthcare, transforming disorganized clinics into data-driven organizations capable of proactive patient management.
