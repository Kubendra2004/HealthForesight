# CHAPTER 6: RESULTS AND DISCUSSIONS

The proposed HealthForesight system successfully demonstrated reliable real-time data handling and intelligent decision support through seamless integration of clinical data, operational workflows, and predictive analytics. Patient information, clinical indicators, and hospital operational data such as admissions, bed occupancy, ICU utilization, and inventory levels were efficiently collected through role-based web interfaces. The FastAPI backend ensured stable and low-latency data transmission, while the hybrid database architecture using MySQL and MongoDB provided secure, consistent, and structured data storage. During testing, the system handled concurrent user interactions without data loss or performance degradation, confirming the robustness of the data acquisition and management process.

After preprocessing the collected healthcare data, machine learning models were employed to analyze clinical patterns and predict patient outcomes. The Random Forest, Logistic Regression, and Gradient Boosting models effectively estimated readmission risk, ICU transfer likelihood, and expected length of stay by considering multiple patient-specific factors simultaneously. The integration of SHAP-based explainability enabled transparent interpretation of predictions, clearly highlighting the most influential clinical features for each outcome. Experimental evaluation showed that the models delivered accurate and consistent predictions across varied patient profiles, and the results were instantly reflected on the doctor dashboard, supporting informed and timely clinical decision-making.

In addition to clinical prediction, the system demonstrated strong performance in hospital resource forecasting and operational support. Prophet-based time-series models accurately predicted short-term demand for beds, ICU capacity, and oxygen supply, allowing administrators to anticipate resource shortages in advance. The real-time dashboards effectively visualized forecasts, occupancy trends, and risk indicators, improving situational awareness.

Furthermore, the **Generative AI and Accessibility layers** proved highly effective during validation. The **RAG-based Chatbot** (powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash) successfully retrieved context-specific hospital protocols with high accuracy, reducing the time required for staff to verify guidelines compared to manual document searches. The **Voice-Enabled Interface** accurately captured spoken commands for navigation and basic data entry, validating its potential to reduce the administrative burden on non-technical staff.

Overall, HealthForesight exhibited fast response times, reliable predictive accuracy, and effective integration of data collection, machine learning, forecasting, visualization, and specialized AI interaction tools, validating its suitability as an intelligent, real-time healthcare management and decision-support system.
